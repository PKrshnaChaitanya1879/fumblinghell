Grvty

A photo colour-grading app I directed and AI built, for a photographer friend who edits in Lightroom.

Built with AI coding agents, directed by me.

What it does

  • Describe a look, get a grid

    Type something like "moody black and white, warm sepia highlights" and Grvty renders 4 to 16 versions of the photo side by side. You choose how many.

  • More like this

    Pick the closest version and it grows a new grid around it, bolder or gentler.

  • AI mode

    A small image-adaptive colour-cube network, trained on 1,520 photos retouched by a professional (MIT-Adobe FiveK). It lands about 2 dB closer to the expert edits than the untouched photos.

  • Saves Lightroom presets

    Any look, AI ones included, becomes a preset that shows up straight away in Lightroom and Camera Raw.

  • A full editor

    Lightroom-style light, colour, black-and-white mix, colour grading, curve, vignette and grain, with before and after.

  • RAW and JPEG, in batches

    Runs offline on an ordinary laptop, with no account and no cloud.

  • Liquid-glass interface

    A light theme and a dark theme with a toggle, and a one-click installer.

  • Not yet

    Learning her own editing style from her past edits. That needs a set of her before and after pairs.

How it was built

Alongside physics, I run experiments in how far AI coding agents can be pushed when I set the vision and direct the work. Grvty was built with AI, not by hand. I set the brief, pushed the interface through several redesigns and decided what shipped; AI wrote the code and trained the model. What it shows is scoping, directing and finishing a build.

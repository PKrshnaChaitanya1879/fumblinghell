Research

Research Intern, School of Engineering Sciences & Technology, University of Hyderabad

Under Prof. Dibakar Das · 18 May – 18 June 2026

  • Synthesised lead-free K₀.₄₈Na₀.₅₂NbO₃ (KNN) piezoceramics by the solid-state reaction route, carrying out the synthesis, calcination, pellet pressing and sintering myself.

Propellant bench tests

With four teammates · August 2026 · small PVC casings, 2.5 to 3 cm long

  • Compared two ways of preparing the potassium nitrate/sucrose fuel, with iron(III) oxide as a catalyst: molten fuel poured straight into the casing, and fuel ground for 20 minutes then compressed into it.
  • The poured test failed: the fuel was contaminated by the detergent used as sealant.
  • The ground and compressed grain burned for about 9 s. Slower than ideal because of moisture, though the catalyst produced good sparks.
  • Fix identified: dry for 12 hours at 60 °C and pack more densely.
